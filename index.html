CARE Tools CQI Framework Timeline

Community Care Licensing Inspection Process Project - 2026

Please note: The CARE Tool CQI Framework Timeline is a living document subject to revision as workload priorities and operational conditions evolve. The availability of data underlying each analytical task is contingent upon the completion of inspections by facility type; accordingly, the number of inspections completed within a given period directly governs the feasibility and sequencing of scheduled analyses. As inspection volume accumulates across facility types, the timeline will be updated to reflect current data availability and analytic readiness.
